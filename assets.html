The One Thing Worth Deciding On This Call
Nobody in your market measures anything, and you are the only one with a live test running

Across all five firms we measured there is one Meta pixel and zero conversion tracking. The whole Northamptonshire commercial electrical market runs on impressions.

You are the only one of the five with something to measure right now. A system went live last week producing real enquiries. Install tracking this week and the next four weeks produce a fact. Install it in a month and those four weeks are gone.

That is the decision. Not whether the site needs work — it does, and it is a day — but whether the test you are already paying for gets measured while it is running.

The Order We Would Do It In
  • Form and tappable phone number on every page, this week. Four of your five competitors have no phone link either — it is a gap the whole field shares and the first to close it wins the enquiries lost between reading and dialling.
  • GA4 and conversion tracking the same day, while the lead system is running.
  • Meta description, and a shorter title without the “best” claim.
  • Structured data — four out of four competitors publish it and you do not.
  • Solar gets its own funnel: its own page, capture and tracking. Two solar clients a week will not arrive through the electrical one, because it is a different buyer at a different stage.
  • Clear the duplicate posts, then plan air conditioning as a proper fourth line rather than a page added later.

Five firms in your field. Zero conversion tracking. One live test, and it is yours.

Measure it while it runs and you will be the only electrician in Northamptonshire who knows what works.

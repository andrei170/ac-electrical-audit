Finding 1 — The Timing Is The Problem
WHAT YOU TURNED ON a lead system, last week WHAT IT IS PRODUCING 10-15 enquiries a week YOUR AVERAGE JOB GBP 5,000 to GBP 20,000 WHAT YOUR WEBSITE CAN RECORD ABOUT ANY OF IT Contact form none Click-to-call link none Google Analytics / GA4 none Google Tag Manager none Meta pixel none Conversion tracking none
This is a one-month window and it is open now

A new lead source is only worth what you can prove about it. In four weeks you will be asking whether it paid, and the honest answer will be “it felt busy”. At five to twenty thousand pounds a job, that is an expensive thing to guess about.

Tracking installed this week captures the whole test. Installed in a month, it captures nothing that has already happened and you start the clock again.

And the site itself cannot take an enquiry. Anyone who finds you, reads the page and decides to act has to go and find a number elsewhere. Some will. Not all.

Finding 2 — Three Business Lines, One Of Them Silent
WHAT YOU RUN OR WANT TO RUN Electrical live commercial + industrial + domestic 10-15 enquiries/week from the new system Solar page exists target: 2 clients a week no form, no number, no way to enquire Air conditioning planned running early next year nothing on the site at all
Solar and electrical are not the same buyer and will not come from the same funnel

Your electrical enquiries come from people who already know they need an electrician. A solar buyer is earlier — they are weighing a purchase, comparing payback, and reading before they call. That is a different page, a different message and a different measurement.

You have a /solar page already, which is further ahead than most. What it does not have is a way to act on it. Two solar clients a week is a real target, and it needs its own capture and its own tracking or you will not know whether it is working.

Air conditioning is the same shape again and it is worth building once, properly, so the third line does not repeat the first two.

Finding 3 — Four Competitors, Four Sets Of Structured Data, And Yours Is The Empty One
STRUCTURED DATA (schema) META DESCRIPTION Thorn Electrical yes yes CAM Electrical yes yes Energy Electrical yes yes ST Electrics yes yes AC Electrical (you) none none YOUR TITLE, 102 CHARACTERS "AC Electrical - Northamptonshire's best local electrician | Commercial | Industrial..." Search results cut around 60. Everything after "local" disappears.
Both of these are an afternoon, and one of them carries a risk

No meta description means Google writes your search snippet itself, picking a sentence from the page. Every competitor controls theirs and you do not control yours.

“Northamptonshire's best local electrician” is a superiority claim with nothing cited behind it, and it is the first thing in your title. It is also the part that survives truncation while your services do not. We would replace it with something specific — commercial and industrial, the towns you cover, and what you actually do.

Finding 4 — The Same Article, Published Three Times
/2024/05/25/the-bright-choice-...-in-your-home-2 /2024/05/25/the-bright-choice-...-in-your-home-2-2 /2024/05/25/the-bright-choice-...-in-your-home-2-2-2 Three URLs. One article. The "-2" suffix is what WordPress adds when a post is saved again under a title it already has.
Small, but it is the tell

Nobody has looked at the back of this site in a while. Three copies of one LED article, plus an author archive at /author/acelectr and a category page, all sitting in the link structure.

It is not costing you jobs on its own. It is worth clearing because duplicates compete with each other, and because it suggests the site has been left rather than run.

What Is Already Working
997 words ahead of Energy Electrical and ST Electrics 8 H2 subheadings joint most of the five firms measured Canonical tag present and correct Commercial + industrial stated in the title, not buried A /solar page already before you have even started selling it Real service depth services, about, news, contact, solar 2 full-time + 2 contractors capacity to take more work now
Why this is a D and not an F

The content and structure are competitive. You are mid-field on words and joint-top on subheadings, which is the hard part. What is missing is everything around the content.

Nothing here is a rebuild. A form, a phone link, a description, schema and a tracking tag is one day of work, and it is the day that makes the next month measurable.

The Order We Would Do It In
  • Form and click-to-call on every page, this week. While the new lead system is running, not after.
  • GA4 and conversion tracking, same day. So the next four weeks of that system produce an answer rather than an impression.
  • Meta description and a shorter title without the “best” claim.
  • Structured data — name, address, phone, service area. All four competitors have it.
  • Build the solar page into its own funnel with its own capture and its own tracking, because two solar clients a week will not come from the electrical one.
  • Clear the duplicate posts, then plan air conditioning as a fourth section rather than a fourth afterthought.

The lead system is live. The measurement window is open now, not next month.

At five to twenty thousand a job, guessing whether it worked is the expensive option.

This is the opening, and it is worth more to you than to any of them

Not one firm in this comparison can tell which page or which ad produced an enquiry. The entire Northamptonshire commercial electrical market is running on impressions.

You are the one with something to measure. A lead system went live last week producing ten to fifteen enquiries a week. None of your competitors has a live test running. If you install tracking now, you are the only firm in this field who will know something factual in a month's time.

That advantage is available today and it costs almost nothing. It also stops being available the moment one of them thinks of it.

Finding 2 — Thorn And CAM Beat You On Content And Lose On Structure
Thorn Electrical 1,078 words · 1 subheading · schema · no phone link CAM Electrical 1,057 words · 1 subheading · schema · phone link AC Electrical 997 words · 8 subheadings
Eighty more words and seven fewer signposts

Thorn publishes a thousand words under a single subheading. A buyer skimming that page on a phone has to read all of it to find out whether they handle factories.

Your page is already the better-built one. Eight sections, each doing a job. That is the part most firms never get right, and you have it.

So the content work here is small. The gap is the plumbing around it, not the writing.

Finding 3 — Only One Of Five Lets You Phone Them From The Page

A shared weakness is still a weakness, and this one is free to fix

Four of five firms make a commercial buyer go and find a number. That is a genuinely poor standard across the whole field, and the first firm to fix it takes the enquiries that would otherwise be lost between reading and dialling.

For an industrial or commercial buyer this matters more than it does for a homeowner. They are often on site, on a phone, with a problem. A tappable number is the whole difference.

What This Field Tells Us To Do
  • Add the description and the schema. Four out of four competitors have both. These are the two columns where you are outright last and they are an afternoon.
  • Put a form and a tappable number on every page and take the one gap the entire field shares.
  • Install tracking while the lead system is live. Nobody else in this comparison is measuring, and nobody else has a running test to measure.
  • Leave the content alone for now. You are mid-field on words and joint-top on structure. Spend the time on the plumbing instead.
  • Give solar its own page, capture and tracking, because none of these four is competing for it seriously.

Five firms, zero conversion tracking, and you are the only one running a live test.

Measure it now and you are the only electrician in Northamptonshire who knows what works.
